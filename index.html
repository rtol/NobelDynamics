Academic Genealogy of Laureates of the Nobel Memorial Prize in the Economic Sciences


The 101 Nobelists are all academically related, some closely, some more distantly. An academic relationship is that between a PhD advisor and candidate. We here group them in 14 families and show the relationships within and between those families.

The accompanying paper is about much more than academic genealogy. It includes friends and family, co-students, co-authors, co-workers, co-editors, publications, citations, specialization, age, nationality, ethnicity, alma mater, faith, and attractiveness. The paper finds that the Nobel Memorial Prize rotates semi-regularly between fields of economics. The path to ultimate success is clear: Write a few highly-cited papers. Find an older co-author who will go on to win the Nobel Prize. Have a student win before you.

There is code to replicate the paper.

The data are available.
