8 lineages: 70 laureates of the Nobel Memorial Prize in the Economic Sciences

The laureates traced back along lines of PhD advisors to a handful of root scholars, one colour per lineage; an alternative to the 14 families. An arrow runs from advisor to candidate. A box in several colours belongs to several lineages. Laureates are in bold with the year of their prize. Scholars who lead to no laureate are left out.

Together, the 8 lineages and the 5 lineages cover 96 of the 101 laureates. Ragnar Frisch, Trygve Haavelmo and Christopher Pissarides (only distantly connected) and Daniel Kahneman and Elinor Ostrom (connected through long detours) are left out; the strays page shows how they connect.

Roots: Wassily Leontief, Kenneth Arrow, Jan Tinbergen, John Maynard Keynes, John Bates Clark, Laurence Laughlin, Edwin Cannan and Theodore Schultz. The other 26 laureates are in the 5 lineages.
