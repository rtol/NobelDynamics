The prize committee and its academic lineages

The members of the committee for the Prize in Economic Sciences, 1969 to 2026, with their PhD advisors and the advisors of those, as far as known. Members are in gold boxes, with their years on the committee (chair and member alike). Laureates are in bold with the year of their prize; candidates in italics with their years of candidacy. An arrow runs from advisor to candidate.

43 members. Many descend from Karl Gustav Cassel, through Myrdal, Ohlin, Lundberg, Svennilson and Åkerman, or from the statistician Harald Cramér; several committee members were each other's advisor and student. Others come from abroad (Friedman, Lucas, Phelps, Solow, Hahn and Gorman, Bhagwati) or from philosophy, psychology, history and political science.
