Strays: the five laureates outside the lineages

Ragnar Frisch, Trygve Haavelmo, Christopher Pissarides, Daniel Kahneman and Elinor Ostrom are left out of the 8 lineages and the 5 lineages, because they join the other laureates only distantly. The figure shows, for each of them, the shortest connection to another laureate, following links between PhD advisor and candidate in either direction. An arrow runs from advisor to candidate. Laureates are in bold with the year of their prize; the laureates they connect to are in grey boxes.

Frisch was Haavelmo's advisor. Both connect through John Humphries, who was also a student of James Heckman: Frisch and Haavelmo both advised Tore Thonstad, whose line runs through Steinar Strøm and Magne Mogstad to Humphries (five steps). Pissarides (nine steps), Kahneman (seven) and Ostrom (twelve) all connect through the philosopher John Dewey, advisor of Wesley Clair Mitchell, who was advisor of Simon Kuznets.
