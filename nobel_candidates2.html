With candidates, page 2: lineages of Edwin Cannan, Carl Menger, Ludwig Brentano and Henry Moore

The laureates together with the candidates: economists who could have won the prize, or still could. Candidates are in italics, with their years of candidacy: from the year they turned 40, or from 1969, the first year of the prize, if later, to the year of their death. Laureates are in bold with the year of their prize. An arrow runs from PhD advisor to candidate. Box colour shows the lineage; a box in several colours belongs to several. Scholars who lead to neither a laureate nor a candidate are left out.

The 6 pages together show all laureates and candidates. The lineages differ from the 8 and 5 lineages: they were chosen to cover the candidates too. A lineage is shown in full, so some scholars appear on more than one page. This page: 165 scholars, 35 laureates, 72 candidates.
